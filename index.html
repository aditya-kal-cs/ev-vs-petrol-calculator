Assumptions and limits

Prices and CO₂ factors are early-October 2026 estimates from public sources (AAA, EIA, Ofgem, RAC, BDEW, EPA, Ember) and change often. Car efficiency, yearly distance and the extra purchase price are rough assumptions: replace them with your own.

Petrol CO₂ counts only the exhaust. EV CO₂ counts only the electricity used. Neither includes building the car or battery, fuel production, or public charging prices, so treat results as estimates, not exact figures. US prices are converted from dollars per gallon.
